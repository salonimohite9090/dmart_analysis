# D-Mart Retail Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing D-Mart retail sales data using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, and business trends through data analysis and an interactive dashboard.

The project uses Excel formulas, PivotTables, charts, and KPI calculations to organize raw sales data into meaningful business information. The dashboard helps explore performance across regions, cities, product categories, customer types, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, net revenue, and profit.
* Compare sales performance across regions and cities.
* Evaluate product category performance.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Understand sales distribution by customer type and payment method.
* Examine discounts and their relationship with profitability.
* Present key business metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **15,345 transaction records** covering January 2023 to December 2024.

It includes order details, sales amounts, discounts, quantities, customer information, payment methods, delivery dates, cities, regions, and product categories.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI          |          Value |
| ------------ | -------------: |
| Total Sales  | ₹39,262,877.52 |
| Net Revenue  | ₹29,388,622.50 |
| Total Profit |  ₹6,872,139.81 |

**KPI Definitions:**

* **Total Sales:** Sum of sales amounts across all records.
* **Net Revenue:** Sales after deducting the calculated discount value.
* **Total Profit:** Sum of recorded profit values.

*Note: These figures follow the calculations in the supplied Excel workbook.*

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

Evaluates total sales, net revenue, and profit to understand overall business performance.

### 2. Regional and City Analysis

Explores sales distribution across the East, North, South, and West regions and across different cities.

### 3. Product Category Analysis

Examines sales across Clothing, Electronics, Grocery, Home Care, and Personal Care.

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis

Explores sales patterns over time using order dates and monthly information.

### 5. Customer Analysis

Compares sales across Member, New, and Regular customer types.

### 6. Payment Method Analysis

Analyzes sales across Card, Cash, and UPI payment methods.

### 7. Discount and Profitability Analysis

Examines discount values and recorded profit to investigate differences in profitability across transactions and business segments.
